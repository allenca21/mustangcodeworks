VoltForge

Live on iOS and Google Play  ·  Updated August 29, 2026

A jobsite reference and calculator built for electricians. Transformer sizing with wiring diagrams, load calculations, and a launchpad that gets you to the number fast.

Common questions

  • Are the calculations code-compliant?

    VoltForge is a reference tool, not a substitute for the current NEC or your local amendments. Always verify against the code edition your jurisdiction has adopted.

  • Does it work offline?

    Yes. Every calculator runs on-device with no connection required.

  • Can I use it in dark conditions?

    Yes. Settings has a light and dark theme; dark is easier in a panel room or after sunset.

Still stuck?

Email support@mustangcodeworks.com with your device model, OS version, and what happened. Purchase problems go faster if you attach the store receipt. We answer within two business days.
